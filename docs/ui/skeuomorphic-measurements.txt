padding edge, which was the first tab's left edge. AK drew the first tab's left
contour one pixel outside the tab (on the sheet edge's column), so that column
was clipped: the tab had no left edge while the sheet below had one, and its
top-left contour broke off at the curve.

first tab active, device px:
